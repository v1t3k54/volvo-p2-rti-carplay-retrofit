# Contributing

Corrections, measurements and verified build photos are welcome.

Please include the vehicle model year, RTI hardware revision, donor unit model and the exact configuration tested. Separate direct observations from proposed solutions.

For wiring information, include connector identity, cavity numbering, measurement conditions and supporting vehicle-specific documentation. Wire colours alone are insufficient. Do not describe an untested pinout or splitter as verified.

For functional claims, describe the test and observed result. Mark compatibility with other vehicles or hardware revisions as unverified until tested.

Only submit images and other material you have permission to publish. Remove private information before uploading.
